The Transformer is a sequence-to-sequence model architecture that eschews recurrence and convolutions, relying entirely on self-attention mechanisms to compute representations of input and output sequences. By replacing recurrent layers with multi-head self-attention, the Transformer allows for significantly greater parallelization during training, reducing training time by orders of magnitude compared to recurrent or convolutional architectures. On the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task, the Transformer establishes a new state of the art with a BLEU score of 28.4, improving over previous ensemble results by over 2 BLEU points. On the WMT 2014 English-to-French translation task, the model establishes a new single-model state of the art BLEU score of 41.8 after training for 3.5 days on eight GPUs. The architecture consists of stacked self-attention and point-wise fully connected feed-forward layers in both the encoder and decoder.
