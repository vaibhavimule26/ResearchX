1. WMT 2014 English-to-German (En-De): Standard machine translation benchmark comprising 4.5 million sentence pairs. Byte-pair encoding (BPE) vocabulary of 37,000 tokens shared across source and target languages.
2. WMT 2014 English-to-French (En-Fr): Large-scale machine translation benchmark comprising 36 million sentence pairs. Word-piece vocabulary of 32,000 word pieces.
3. English Constituency Parsing (Penn Treebank - WSJ section 23): Evaluation benchmark assessing the generalization ability of the Transformer architecture to structural grammatical parsing tasks with 40,000 training sentences.
